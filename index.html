Hi, I’m Maliyah

The friendly face behind the brush

I’m a face painter who lives to see a child light up when they catch their reflection for the first time. Every design is painted with gentle, professional-grade paints that are kind to sensitive skin and wash off easily at bath time.

Whether it’s a backyard birthday, a school fete or a big community festival, I bring the colours, the sparkle and a whole lot of patience for the wriggly ones.
